01Pullbox Dimensions

Advanced — offsets, margins & sheet size
Assumed construction: Body = bottom + two side walls developed as one flat piece, running the full Height, with the side-offset added to the two ends of that run: (Height + 2×offset) × (Width + 2×Depth). Box end = top & bottom cap pieces (2 pcs), each the Width×Depth cross-section plus offset on all four sides — unaffected by Pullbox Type. Cover = Width × Height plus offset on all four sides: (Width + 2×offset) × (Height + 2×offset). When Pullbox Type is set to Swing, the "Swing type — extra side offset" above is added on top of the normal side offset for the Body and Cover only (box end caps stay the same). Edit the numbers above if your fold pattern differs.

Fits / sheet and fits / quarter pack a main grid of that piece, then rotate the leftover pieces 90° to fill whatever strip is left over (right side or bottom) — with 15mm side / 15mm top gaps kept between adjacent pieces. This assumes that item is cut on its own, not mixed with other pieces. A shop nesting different parts together on one sheet can usually do even better than the "sheets needed" column below suggests.
